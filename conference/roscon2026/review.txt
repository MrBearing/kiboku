Paper summary
-------------
This proposal presents Kobuki, a tool that is intended to statically analyze ROS workspaces with zero dependencies on ROS binaries or ROS stack.

Comments for authors
--------------------
This tool is an interesting approach for a statically code analysis on ROS workspaces. I think some real-world examples of why this is a problem in the robotics community would help to understand the relevance of these tools and why it is better than other approaches.
